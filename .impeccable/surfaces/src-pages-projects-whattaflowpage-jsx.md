# Whattaflow case study

Mode: Experience. Scope: `/projects/whattaflow` and its portfolio entry.

Audience: recruiters, clients, and collaborators evaluating Andrea as both a UI/UX designer and front-end developer. The page must make the problem, judgment, shipped interfaces, and technical ownership legible without inflating the process or inventing outcomes. Primary action: visit the live Whattaflow information site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One platform, two experiences. The hero names and previews both the researcher and participant sides, with detailed UX reasoning unfolding on scroll.

OWN-WORLD: The portfolio’s Archivo and Space Grotesk typography frames real Whattaflow screens. The official Whattaflow wordmark and quiet concentric circles sit on the portfolio's existing theme-aware paper and ink palette.

STORY: Understand the wearable-data problem, see how researcher and participant needs diverge, inspect the UX decisions, then see the design system, implementation, testing, and the promotional site as proof of full ownership.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered official wordmark, “Una piattaforma. Due esperienze.”, a one-sentence explanation, and a short scroll action. Two labeled uncased screenshots show the researcher and participant products over slow concentric circles.

FORM: User requested a simple hero with animated circles, later clarified that both products should be visible and that blue is outside the portfolio palette. Surface seed `cff5fe61`. Signature motion: restrained circle rotation behind static interfaces, disabled under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: bilingual copy, light and dark themes, responsive layouts, visible focus, reduced-motion fallback, real assets only, no invented test statistics or business claims.
